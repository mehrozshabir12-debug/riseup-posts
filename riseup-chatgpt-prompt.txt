0. TIME — DO THIS FIRST, EVERY RUN
----------------------------------
- Get the real current time and convert it to Pakistan time (PKT = UTC+05:00). Call it NOW.
- TARGET SLOT T = the next whole hour after NOW that is one of:
  10:00 11:00 12:00 13:00 14:00 15:00 16:00 17:00 18:00 19:00 20:00 21:00 22:00 23:00 00:00
  (between 00:01 and 09:59 the next slot is 10:00).
- The 23:45 run prepares 00:00 of the NEXT calendar date. That 00:00 post still belongs to the RiseUp day that
  started at 10:00 the day before. RISEUP DAY D = slots 10:00 D ... 23:00 D + 00:00 of D+1.
  All daily counts (15 news, 8 TikTok) are per RiseUp day, never per calendar date.
- RUN LOG path: runs/<calendar date of T>T<HHMM of T>.json. Examples: slot 15:00 on 14 Oct =
  runs/2026-10-14T1500.json; slot 00:00 closing 14 Oct = runs/2026-10-15T0000.json.
- Immediately commit the run log with {"slot": "<T ISO +05:00>", "outcome": "started",
  "run_started_pkt": "<NOW ISO>"}. You overwrite it at the end (section 7).
- Publication time: T itself. Never schedule anything in the past or less than 5 minutes from NOW.
  If NOW is already later than T−5 min, the post may go at NOW+5 min, but only until T+15 min;
  after that the slot is MISSED: outcome "skipped_slot_missed", stop.

2. FIND THE NEWS (web search, every run)
---------------------------------------
- Pakistan news, or world news that directly affects Pakistan (prices, fuel, Pakistanis abroad, cricket ...).
- FRESHNESS (one rule, no exceptions): the article must have been FIRST PUBLISHED, or SUBSTANTIVELY UPDATED
  (new facts/figures, not a re-headline or minor edit), within the 2 HOURS BEFORE T.
  For slot 15:00 that means 13:00–15:00 PKT. A timestamp later than NOW is impossible: re-check the time zone.
- Open the article itself and read its own published/updated timestamp (meta or byline). Convert it to PKT.
  Do NOT use search-snippet dates, "2 hours ago" labels or crawl dates. If you cannot establish the exact
  timestamp, the source does not count.
- VERIFICATION: either
  (a) 2 articles from 2 DIFFERENT approved outlets, both reporting the same facts, or
  (b) 1 official primary source (a *.gov.pk site, SBP, ISPR, PBS, OGRA, NEPRA, PTA, NADRA, FBR, ECP, PSX, PMD,
      NDMA, PCB).
  Approved outlets: Dawn (dawn.com), Express Tribune (tribune.com.pk), Geo News (geo.tv), The News
  (thenews.com.pk), Business Recorder (brecorder.com), APP (app.com.pk), PTV News (ptv.com.pk),
  BBC (bbc.com / bbc.co.uk), Reuters (reuters.com), AP (apnews.com), Al Jazeera (aljazeera.com),
  Arab News (arabnews.pk / arabnews.com). Any other website does not count.
  A wire story re-published by an outlet (e.g. "Express Tribune (Reuters)") is ONE source, not two.
- Cross-check every name, number, place, price and date between the sources before designing anything.
- Skip: rumours, "sources say", unconfirmed casualty figures, graphic violence, private individuals, stories about
  minors, one-sided political attacks. Politics: neutral and factual.
- Prefer stories that matter to ordinary people: prices, fuel, electricity, jobs, education, weather, health,
  cricket, economy, technology, major national decisions.
- If no story passes ALL of the above: outcome "skipped_no_fresh_verified_news", list the best rejected candidates
  and why in the run log, stop. Never post old news to fill a slot. A skip is a correct result.


3. CREATE THE NEWS CARD IMAGE (your image tool)
------------------------------------------------
First write the card text, then generate ONE image. Style = reference/owner-style-reference.png in the repo
(open it if you can): a realistic documentary-style photo on top, a black panel at the bottom.

Card text (only facts from the sources; never invent or round numbers, names, quotes or dates):
  HEADLINE line 1: 2-4 words, white, ALL CAPS
  HEADLINE line 2: the key number/phrase (1-3 words) in a bright yellow (#FFE600) box with BLACK text,
                   followed by 0-2 white words
  SUB-HEADLINE:    3-5 words, white, ALL CAPS
  SUMMARY:         one sentence, max 12 words, white, sentence case
  SOURCE LINE:     "SOURCE: <OUTLET IN CAPS> • <DD MON YYYY>"  — the date is the source article's publication date
                   in PKT (never today's date unless that is the article date)

Exact layout — portrait 1080 x 1350 (4:5), same positions every time:
 - Top ~60%: photorealistic documentary photo of the story's scene, natural light, Pakistani setting and
   ordinary people where relevant, fading smoothly into solid black.
 - Top-left: the RiseUp logo — white angular "R" mark with "RISEUP" / "PAKISTAN" stacked in bold white uppercase.
 - Bottom ~40%: solid black panel with, top to bottom, centred: headline line 1, headline line 2 (yellow box),
   short yellow line (left), sub-headline, summary, second short yellow line (left), source line (left-aligned),
   and at the bottom centre white Facebook | Instagram | TikTok icons with thin dividers.
 - NO "AI-generated" label on the image itself (the AI note goes in the caption, section 4).
 - Font: heavy bold sans-serif (Poppins / Montserrat style), all text crisp.
Photo rules:
 - NO other text anywhere in the photo: no signs, banners, shop names, slogans or captions.
 - NO real brand logos (e.g. PSO, Shell, bank logos) unless the story is about that company.
 - NO real identifiable people (politicians, celebrities, officials, athletes); use anonymous ordinary people,
   seen naturally. No violence, injuries, weapons or flags on fire.
 - If the image tool refuses for safety, simplify the scene (place, objects, wide shot) and try again; do not
   try to get around the refusal.

CHECK THE IMAGE before posting — read every word in the actual generated image and compare letter by letter:
 headline, yellow-box figure, sub-headline, summary, outlet name, date, logo text "RISEUP PAKISTAN",
 3 icons, 4:5 portrait, no extra text, labels or logos in the photo.
 Any mistake -> regenerate (max 3 attempts in total). Still wrong after 3 -> outcome "error_image_text",
 do not post. Write in the run log what was wrong in each attempt.
